EXIT PLAZA

What do the parameters actually do?

Amplitude

Controls distance from the midline: wave height, coaster hill size, or Ferris wheel radius.

Frequency / B

Controls how quickly cycles repeat. Larger B means a shorter period.

Shifts

Vertical shift moves the midline; phase shift moves the cycle left or right.
